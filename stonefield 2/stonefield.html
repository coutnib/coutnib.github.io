Inventory and crafting

Punch trees for logs, craft a workbench, then tools. Craft torches from coal and a stick: they light the dark and keep Gloomlings away. Sleep on a bedroll to skip the night. Every biome has its own villages and creatures, and some creatures are rare: your field journal keeps track. Ember ore hides below layer 16; four shards make a Rift Stone to the Cinderdeep.
